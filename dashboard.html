Key insights

  • Distance is the single biggest driver of price (correlation: 0.65). Duration follows closely (0.54).
  • Clear budget vs full-service airline split — budget carriers (GoFirst, Indigo, AirAsia India, SpiceJet) average ₹10,300–10,900, while full-service international carriers (Qatar Airways, Singapore Airlines, Etihad, British Airways, Emirates) average ~₹99,800–101,000, roughly a 10x gap.
  • More stops correlates with higher prices, but weakly (correlation: 0.11) — ₹61,578 (non-stop) → ₹78,883 (1 stop) → ₹84,656 (2 stops).
  • Booking earlier saves a small amount, but weakly (correlation: -0.096).
  • Passenger count has no relationship with price (correlation: 0.004).

Recommendations for travelers

  • Route matters more than timing — the biggest savings come from route/airline choice.
  • Budget carriers offer dramatic savings on comparable domestic routes.
  • Booking earlier gives a modest edge, but don't expect large savings from timing alone.
  • Don't over-optimize for stops — it's mainly about convenience, not major cost savings.
